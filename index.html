Welcome to Oakridge Contemporary Academy

For over a quarter of a century, Oakridge Academy has fostered academic curiosity, ethical leadership, and creative resilience in students from Kindergarten through Grade 12. We cultivate an inclusive ecosystem where tradition inspires forward-looking scholastic exploration.

flag Our Mission

To ignite lifelong intellectual passion, fostering ethical thinkers who enrich global society through deliberate service and rigorous scholarship.

visibility Our Vision

To define contemporary educational excellence by molding compassionate, adaptable trailblazers prepared for an interconnected world.
